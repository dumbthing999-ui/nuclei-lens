# Track and Category

**Decision date:** 2026-10-08.

- **Track:** Coding.
- **Category:** Biology/Medical and Environmental Science.
- **Project:** NucleiLens — a nuclei-counting review tool for microscopy research and education.

The coded application and reproducible image-analysis pipeline satisfy the intended Coding Track format. Biology is the honest category because the problem, inputs, annotations, and proposed benefit are specific to microscopy measurements. Computer Science + AI is a plausible secondary fit, but category choice follows the domain being served, not perceived entrant counts.

Scope is fluorescence nuclei review on a public cell-line dataset. The project does not diagnose disease, assess patients, or claim treatment benefit.
